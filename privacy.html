Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

The short version: Wondra collects no data. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The Wondra iOS app has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. All of its cards and lessons are built into the app and work offline.

New and corrected cards

About once a day, when you’re online, Wondra downloads one public file from wondraapp.com that can add new cards or fix existing ones. It’s the same file for everyone, and the request carries no account, ID or anything about you or what you’ve read. Like any website, our host (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; we don’t receive or use them. The download is checked before use, and only ever contains cards, never code.

What stays on your phone

To work, Wondra keeps a few things on your device only:

  • which cards you’ve seen, and the ones you saved
  • how many ideas you’ve revealed each day
  • which hand-built lessons you’ve completed
  • a simple record of the subjects you tend to explore, used to shape your rounds
  • your reactions to cards (mind blown, wow, I knew this) and the guides you follow
  • your daily reminder time, if you turn the reminder on

We can’t see any of this, and it isn’t sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it. You can reset what Wondra has learned about your interests at any time in the Explore tab.

Notifications

The daily reminder is off unless you turn it on. It’s scheduled on your phone by iOS; no server is involved. You can turn it off in Wondra or in your iPhone’s Settings.

Sharing

If you share a card or a link to Wondra, it goes only to the app you pick in your phone’s share sheet. Wondra doesn’t see what you share or who you share it with.

Children

Wondra collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device (for example, AI-written lessons for topics you type in), we’ll update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released, and explain exactly what is sent and to whom.
