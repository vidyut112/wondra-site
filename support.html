Support

Something not working, or spotted a mistake in a card? We’d like to hear.

Email: support@wondraapp.com. We usually reply within a few days.

How does a round work?

Each round is ten idea cards. Take a guess (“I think I know” or “No idea”), then read a short answer. After ten cards the round ends on purpose. You can start ten more whenever you like, but there’s no endless feed.

Why do some saved cards come back?

Cards you save return a few times over the following weeks (after about 1, 3, 7 and 21 days) as a quick “what was the answer?”. Recalling something is one of the best ways to make it stick. Other cards don’t repeat.

How does Wondra choose my cards?

It notices what you save and explore, and leans each round a little toward those subjects, while always mixing in new ground. This happens entirely on your phone. To see or reset it, open Explore and scroll to What your scroll leans toward.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Open Explore → A daily reminder. Pick a time and tap Turn on, or tap Turn off. If nothing arrives, check that notifications for Wondra are allowed in your iPhone’s Settings.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. Every card and lesson is built into the app.

I think a card is wrong

Accuracy matters a lot to us, and every card has been fact-checked, but mistakes can slip through. Email us the card’s question and what you think is wrong (a source helps), and we’ll check it and fix it in the next update.

How do I delete my data?

Wondra keeps your progress only on your phone; we never receive it. Deleting the app deletes everything. To reset only your interests, use the reset button in Explore.
