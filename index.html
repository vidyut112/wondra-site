Hard ideas, a spark at a time.

Ten surprising ideas a day from maths, science, philosophy, economics, language and the arts. Take a guess, see a plain-language answer, and after ten cards it stops, on purpose.

Meet your guides

Ten guides present the ideas, each with their own taste: Tally the counter, Twist the puzzler, Bolt the tinkerer, Quill the sceptic, Nova the stargazer and more. Follow the ones you like and your rounds lean their way, with new ground always mixed in.

Go deeper

Tap “Go deeper” on hundreds of cards for a short lesson with an everyday analogy, four steps and a diagram, even offline. Many have something to play with: the Monty Hall game, a Galton board building a bell curve, a race between sorting algorithms, a code that fixes its own errors, a speed dial that slows time.
